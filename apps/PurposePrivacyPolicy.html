Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies to the Purpose app (hereby referred to as "Application") for mobile devices that was created by progdamn (hereby referred to as "Service Provider") as a Free service. This service is intended for use "AS IS".


Information Collection and Use

The Application does not collect any information about you and does not send any information anywhere. It has no permission to access the Internet, contains no advertising, analytics or crash reporting, and has no accounts.

Everything the Application works with is created on your device and stays on it:

  • the list of apps you chose to track;
  • the goals you write and the time you plan for each visit;
  • when you opened a tracked app, how long you stayed, and your answer on whether the goal was met;
  • the Application's settings.

This information is kept in the Application's private storage on your device. The Service Provider has no access to it. Nothing is recorded about apps you do not track.


Accessibility Service

The Application uses the Android Accessibility Service API, and only for one purpose: to know which app is open on the screen. When the window on the screen changes, the Application reads which app is now in front (its package name). It does not read the content of the screen, what you type, or your messages.

This is used to show the goal screen when you open an app you track, to count the time you spend in it, and to close the notification shade when you tap Finish in the Application's notification. This information is processed on your device and is never transmitted.

The Application explains this and asks for your consent before you turn the service on. You can turn it off at any time in the system accessibility settings.


Other Permissions
  • Visibility of apps that have a launcher icon — to let you choose which apps to track.
  • Display over other apps — to open the goal screen on top of a tracked app.
  • Notifications — for the session stopwatch and the question about your goal.
  • Run at startup — to restore the state of an open session after the device restarts.

Export

If you turn on auto-export, the Application writes a file with your data to a folder you choose on your device, and can import such a file back. The file goes only where you put it. Android cloud backup is turned off for the Application.


Third Party Access

The Application does not share any information with third parties and contains no third-party services that collect data.


Opt-Out Rights

You can stop all use of information by the Application easily by uninstalling it. You may use the standard uninstall processes as may be available as part of your mobile device or via the mobile application marketplace or network.


Data Retention and Deletion

Your data stays on your device until you delete it. Uninstalling the Application, or clearing its storage in the system settings, deletes all of it. An export file you created stays in the folder you chose until you delete it yourself. Since the Service Provider never receives any of your data, there is nothing for them to keep or delete on your behalf.


Children

The Application is not directed to children under the age of 13. It does not collect personally identifiable information from anyone, including children.


Security

Because your data never leaves your device through the Application, it is protected by the security of your device itself: its screen lock and its storage encryption.


Changes

You are advised to consult this Privacy Policy regularly for any changes, as continued use is deemed approval of all changes.
